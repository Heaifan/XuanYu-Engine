# XYE-SRP-R1-B0 migration risk audit

- Highest-risk migration surface is Render/Session because the current contract is Present-based: `Scene3dFrameRoute` promotes pending data only after successful Present, while `VulkanScene3dRenderFrameSnapshot` publishes Camera/Overlay snapshots only after Present. A migration that publishes new snapshots before Present can desynchronize Picking/Gizmo from the visible frame.
- Mode risks are caused by distributed mutable flags: `MoveToolActive`, `BlenderMoveActive`, ground placement, navigation drag, and transform drag can overlap. Preserve explicit precedence and cancel/cleanup transitions; do not replace them with independently synchronized booleans.
- Input risks are event-order and ownership risks: raw pointer/key events, overlay navigation events, scene-tool events, picking, ground hover, and focus loss are wired separately. Reordering or broadening a route can cause overlay clicks to fall through, scene tools to consume navigation input, or capture loss to leave stale drag state.
- Transform risks are stale-reference and mixed-authority risks: `TransformStartSnapshot` freezes Camera, Gizmo, initial transform, and dirty state; `TransformDragRoute` uses that frozen camera for the drag. Substituting current CameraSnapshot/RenderScene during a drag changes geometry and cancel semantics.
- Render risks include RenderSeq/camera revision mismatch, stale PresentedPick/Persistent Gizmo state, resize fallback to Clear, and changing DrawList ownership. Keep SceneState, Camera, Terrain, and Presented products versioned independently until convergence.
- Session risks include stale UI callbacks after Dispose/Reinitialize, swapchain recreation, resource lifetime, and failure-state transitions. Preserve `SessionGeneration` checks and old Presented snapshots on failed frames.
